# Mission

Project Orion is a deterministic AI-assisted decision-support platform for short-term equity trading.

Its purpose is **not** to predict future market prices.

Its purpose is to continuously identify deterministic trading opportunities where predefined technical, risk and portfolio conditions justify opening or maintaining a position.

Every recommendation generated by Orion must always remain:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Reproducible
- Testable
- Transparent

Artificial Intelligence never creates trading decisions.

Artificial Intelligence only explains deterministic output produced by Orion.

---

# Primary Objectives

For every monitored instrument Orion continuously answers six questions.

1. Is this instrument worth monitoring?

2. Is this an appropriate moment to enter?

3. How large should the position be?

4. Should a trade be opened?

5. Should an existing trade remain open?

6. Is it time to exit?

Mission Control presents these answers as quickly and clearly as possible.

---

# Trading Philosophy

Project Orion follows several non-negotiable principles.

- Capital preservation comes first.
- Missing a profitable trade is acceptable.
- Large losses are unacceptable.
- Small repeatable gains are preferred over infrequent large gains.
- Every trade requires a predefined exit strategy.
- Every trade has a known maximum loss.
- Every recommendation must be deterministic.
- Every recommendation must remain explainable.
- Architecture has priority over speed.
- Duplicate business logic is forbidden.

# Deterministic Trading Flow

Project Orion currently contains three deterministic workflows.

Every workflow is completely independent.

Every workflow has exactly one deterministic decision engine.

Artificial Intelligence never participates in these decisions.

---

## 1. Opportunity Workflow

```text
Universe

↓

YahooProvider

↓

TechnicalScanner

↓

AnalysisEngine

↓

MarketScanner

↓

TradingPipeline

↓

OpportunityService

↓

Mission Control
```

Mission Control is responsible only for presenting deterministic opportunities.

TradingPipeline remains the only source of BUY / HOLD / SELL opportunity decisions.

No UI component may alter these decisions.

---

## 2. Entry Workflow

The Entry Workflow determines whether a new position should be opened.

The TradingPipeline remains the only deterministic decision engine responsible for BUY, HOLD and SELL recommendations.

```text
Symbol

↓

YahooProvider

↓

IndicatorBuilder

↓

TradingPipeline

↓

BUY / HOLD / SELL

↓

Trading Workspace
```

The Trading Workspace presents deterministic output only.

It never performs calculations.

It never generates trading decisions.

Artificial Intelligence may explain the TradingPipeline output but may never modify it.

---

## Trading Decision

Every TradingPipeline result contains deterministic information.

Typical outputs include

- Decision
- Confidence
- Risk
- Pressure Score
- Position Size
- Human-readable Explanation

Only BUY decisions may continue into the Trade Lifecycle.

HOLD and SELL remain informational decisions.

---

## Open Trade Workflow

Sprint 5.5 introduces the first implementation of Orion's Trade Lifecycle.

The workflow is intentionally explicit.

A BUY recommendation never creates a trade automatically.

The user must explicitly confirm opening a trade.

Current workflow

```text
Trading Workspace

↓

BUY

↓

Open Trade

↓

TradeLifecycleService

↓

OpenTradeStore

↓

Trade Monitor
```

This prevents accidental trade creation.

It also reflects real-world trading where the executed broker price may differ from the analysed market price.

---

## TradeLifecycleService

TradeLifecycleService owns the lifecycle state of every trade.

Responsibilities

- Open trades
- Maintain trade status
- Forward closed trades
- Coordinate lifecycle transitions

TradeLifecycleService never calculates BUY decisions.

TradeLifecycleService never calculates EXIT decisions.

It only manages deterministic lifecycle state.

---

## OpenTradeStore

OpenTradeStore owns persistence of all active trades.

Responsibilities

- Save open trades
- Load open trades
- Remove closed trades

OpenTradeStore does not calculate indicators.

OpenTradeStore does not perform analysis.

OpenTradeStore only persists lifecycle data.

---

## Trade Monitor

Trade Monitor is the operational workspace for active trades.

Its purpose is to continuously answer

- Is this trade healthy?
- Should this trade remain open?
- Has risk increased?
- Should action be taken?

Trade Monitor combines

- open trade information
- deterministic technical analysis
- exit intelligence
- lifecycle state

into a single operational workspace.

---

## 3. Exit Workflow

The Exit Workflow determines whether an existing position should remain open.

Unlike the Entry Workflow, it never creates BUY decisions.

Its responsibility is to protect capital while allowing profitable trades to continue.

Current deterministic workflow

```text
Trade

↓

TradeMonitorService

↓

PositionAnalysisService

↓

YahooProvider

↓

AnalysisEngine

↓

ExitEvaluationService

↓

Trade Monitor
```

Trade Monitor presents deterministic output only.

It never performs calculations itself.

---

## PositionAnalysisService

PositionAnalysisService retrieves all information required to evaluate an existing trade.

Responsibilities

- Retrieve latest market price
- Retrieve historical candles
- Build deterministic analysis input
- Reuse AnalysisEngine

PositionAnalysisService performs no trading decisions.

---

## AnalysisEngine

AnalysisEngine is shared infrastructure.

The same deterministic analysis engine supports both

- Entry decisions
- Exit decisions

AnalysisEngine produces technical scores only.

Typical outputs include

- Trend Score
- Momentum Score
- Volatility Score
- Relative Strength
- Structure Score
- Overall Technical Score

AnalysisEngine never produces

- BUY
- SELL
- EXIT

Those decisions belong to higher-level deterministic services.

---

## ExitEvaluationService

ExitEvaluationService is the only deterministic source of exit advice.

Possible deterministic outputs include

- HOLD_POSITION
- TAKE_PROFIT
- STOP_LOSS
- EXIT_DUE_TO_WEAKNESS
- TRAILING_STOP
- EXIT_DUE_TO_TIME_LIMIT

Future versions may introduce additional deterministic exit categories without changing the architectural responsibility.

---

## Exit Intelligence

Exit Intelligence translates deterministic exit analysis into information that can be interpreted quickly by the trader.

Current information includes

- Exit Score
- Trend Status
- Momentum Status
- Risk Status
- Exit Reasons

Future versions may additionally display

- Holding Days
- Maximum Favorable Excursion
- Maximum Adverse Excursion
- Portfolio Exposure
- Dynamic Stop Recommendations

Exit Intelligence never creates trading decisions.

It only presents deterministic output.

---

## Position Monitoring

Every open trade is continuously monitored.

Current monitoring includes

- Entry Price
- Current Price
- Unrealized Profit/Loss
- Market Value
- Stop Loss
- Take Profit
- Exit Score
- Exit Reasons
- Technical Status

Future monitoring will include

- Trailing Stop
- Time in Trade
- Portfolio Risk
- Portfolio Allocation
- Performance Statistics

The monitoring process remains fully deterministic.

Artificial Intelligence only explains the deterministic analysis.

---

# Risk Management

Risk management is integrated into every deterministic decision.

Every proposed trade should eventually contain

- Entry Price
- Current Price
- Stop Loss
- Take Profit
- Position Size
- Expected Risk
- Expected Reward
- Risk / Reward Ratio

Trades failing deterministic minimum requirements should never enter the Trade Lifecycle.

Risk management always has priority over trade frequency.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is an explainability layer.

AI may

- Explain trading decisions
- Summarize deterministic analysis
- Compare opportunities
- Generate natural-language explanations
- Produce daily market summaries
- Produce portfolio summaries

AI may never

- Generate BUY signals
- Generate SELL signals
- Generate EXIT signals
- Calculate indicators
- Calculate confidence
- Calculate position size
- Override deterministic output
- Override TradingPipeline
- Override ExitEvaluationService
- Open trades
- Close trades

The deterministic backend always remains the single source of truth.
