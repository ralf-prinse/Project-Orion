# Mission

Project Orion is a deterministic decision-support platform for short-term equity trading.

Its purpose is **not** to predict the future.

Its purpose is to continuously identify situations where the probability of a successful trade justifies taking action within predefined deterministic risk limits.

Every recommendation must be:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Reproducible
- Testable

Artificial Intelligence never creates trading decisions.

Artificial Intelligence explains deterministic output generated by Orion.

---

# Primary Objectives

For every monitored symbol Orion continuously answers four questions.

1. Is this instrument worth monitoring?

2. Is this an appropriate moment to enter?

3. Should an existing position be maintained?

4. Is it time to exit?

Mission Control presents these answers as quickly and clearly as possible.

---

# Trading Philosophy

Project Orion follows several non-negotiable principles.

- Capital preservation comes first.
- Missing a trade is acceptable.
- Large losses are unacceptable.
- Small repeatable gains are preferred over infrequent large gains.
- Every trade requires a predefined exit strategy.
- Every trade has a known maximum loss.
- Deterministic consistency is more important than trade frequency.

# Deterministic Trading Flow

Project Orion now contains two deterministic decision workflows.

---

## Entry Decision Workflow

```text
Universe

↓

YahooProvider

↓

TechnicalScanner

↓

AnalysisEngine

↓

MarketScanner

↓

TradingPipeline

↓

OpportunityService

↓

Mission Control

↓

Trading Workspace
```

TradingPipeline remains the only source of BUY / HOLD / SELL decisions.

---

## Exit Decision Workflow

```text
Trade

↓

PositionAnalysisService

↓

AnalysisEngine

↓

PositionMonitorService

↓

ExitEvaluationService

↓

Trade Monitor (Position Monitor)
```

ExitEvaluationService remains the only source of deterministic exit decisions.

Mission Control and Trade Monitor only present deterministic output.

# Entry Requirements

A BUY candidate should satisfy multiple independent deterministic conditions.

Typical categories include

Liquidity

- sufficient average daily volume

Spread

- acceptable bid / ask spread

Trend

- alignment with prevailing trend

Momentum

- positive short-term momentum

Volatility

- sufficient movement without excessive instability

Relative Strength

- outperform benchmark

Market Context

- favourable overall market condition

News

- no significant adverse news

No single indicator is sufficient.

Signals require deterministic agreement between multiple factors.

# Trade Lifecycle Strategy

Project Orion now supports two independent deterministic decision moments.

## Entry Strategy

The Entry Strategy determines whether a new trade should be opened.

Decision engine

TradingPipeline

Outputs

- BUY
- HOLD
- SELL

---

## Exit Strategy

The Exit Strategy determines whether an existing trade should remain open.

Decision engine

ExitEvaluationService

Outputs

- HOLD_POSITION
- TAKE_PROFIT
- STOP_LOSS
- EXIT_DUE_TO_WEAKNESS

Both strategies reuse the same deterministic AnalysisEngine.

Only the interpretation differs.

This prevents duplicated technical-analysis logic while allowing independent optimisation of entry and exit behaviour.
