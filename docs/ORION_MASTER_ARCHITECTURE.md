# Purpose

This document is the single architectural source of truth for Project Orion.

All architectural decisions, design principles, rendering pipelines, backend orchestration and workspace composition are defined here.

Other documentation may reference this document but must never duplicate architectural specifications.

---

# Vision

Project Orion is a deterministic AI-assisted desktop trading platform focused on short-term trading of highly liquid equities.

The platform continuously analyses market data, identifies deterministic trading opportunities, monitors active positions and explains every decision transparently.

Artificial Intelligence never creates trading decisions.

Artificial Intelligence only explains deterministic outputs.

---

# Core Philosophy

Orion is built around five non-negotiable principles.

## 1. Deterministic First

Every BUY, HOLD, SELL or EXIT signal must originate from deterministic backend services.

Signals must be:

- deterministic
- reproducible
- explainable
- testable
- traceable

Random behaviour is forbidden.

---

## 2. AI Explainability

Artificial Intelligence never:

- buys
- sells
- calculates indicators
- sizes positions
- determines confidence
- generates risk scores

AI explains deterministic output generated by backend services.

---

## 3. Separation of Responsibilities

Every architectural layer owns exactly one responsibility.

```
Business Services
        ↓
Presenters
        ↓
Presentation Models
        ↓
Workspace Renderer
        ↓
Widgets
        ↓
ChartCanvasBuilder
        ↓
ChartCanvas
        ↓
Chart Layers
        ↓
Qt Painter
```

Responsibilities never overlap.

---

## 4. Architecture Before Features

Long-term maintainability always has priority over short-term implementation speed.

Reusable architecture is preferred over feature-specific implementations.

Duplicate implementations are forbidden.

---

## 5. Mission Control

Mission Control becomes the operational center of Orion.

The objective is not to display charts.

The objective is to present deterministic market intelligence as quickly as possible.

The user should immediately understand:

- current market state
- best opportunities
- active positions
- required actions
- current risks
