# Vision

Project Orion is a deterministic AI-assisted desktop trading workstation.

Its objective is **not** to predict financial markets.

Its objective is to continuously discover deterministic opportunities, analyse individual symbols, size positions, manage open trades and explain every recommendation through transparent reasoning.

Artificial Intelligence never creates trading decisions.

Artificial Intelligence only explains deterministic output generated by Orion.

Mission Control is the operational center of Orion.

Trade Monitor is the operational center of the Trade Lifecycle.

The complete deterministic workflow is now:

```text
Market Awareness

↓

Opportunity Discovery

↓

Trading Decision

↓

Position Sizing

↓

Open Trade

↓

Trade Lifecycle

↓

Trade Monitor

↓

Exit Intelligence

↓

Trade History

↓

Paper Trading

↓

Optional Broker Connectivity
```

Every stage remains deterministic.

Every stage remains explainable.

Every stage remains independently testable.

# Core Philosophy

Project Orion is built around seven non-negotiable architectural principles.

---

## 1. Deterministic First

Every BUY, HOLD, SELL and EXIT recommendation originates exclusively from deterministic backend services.

Signals must always remain

- deterministic
- reproducible
- explainable
- testable
- traceable

Random behaviour is forbidden.

---

## 2. AI Explainability

Artificial Intelligence may

- explain
- summarize
- compare
- translate deterministic output into natural language

Artificial Intelligence may never

- generate BUY decisions
- generate SELL decisions
- generate EXIT decisions
- calculate indicators
- calculate confidence
- calculate position size
- calculate risk
- override deterministic output
- open trades
- close trades

AI always executes after deterministic backend services have completed.

---

## 3. Separation of Responsibilities

Every architectural layer owns exactly one responsibility.

```text
Providers

↓

Deterministic Services

↓

Controllers

↓

Presenters

↓

View Models

↓

Workspaces

↓

Widgets
```

Responsibilities never overlap.

Business logic never enters the UI.

Rendering never enters backend services.

Controllers orchestrate only.

Presenters format only.

Workspaces render only.

---

## 4. Architecture Before Features

Architecture always has priority over implementation speed.

Reusable components are preferred over feature-specific implementations.

Duplicate business logic is forbidden.

Every new feature must fit inside the existing architecture before implementation begins.

---

## 5. Mission Control

Mission Control is the operational center of Orion.

Within seconds the trader must understand

- current market condition
- strongest opportunities
- available trading capital
- FX-aware buying power
- recommended position size
- active risks
- required actions

Mission Control never performs calculations.

Mission Control presents deterministic output only.

---

# Service Design Rules

Every service owns one responsibility.

Services never render UI.

Services never own Qt objects.

Services never call presenters.

Services communicate through deterministic domain models.

No duplicated service responsibilities are permitted.

AnalysisEngine remains shared infrastructure.

TradingPipeline remains the single entry decision engine.

ExitEvaluationService remains the single exit decision engine.

TradeLifecycleService remains the lifecycle owner.

The backend remains the single source of truth.

# Development Rules

Every implementation inside Project Orion must satisfy the following architectural rules.

Trading decisions must always originate from deterministic backend services.

No user interface component may calculate

- Position Size
