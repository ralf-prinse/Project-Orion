# Strategy S2.1 — Opportunity Ranking

## Goal
Rank all scanned opportunities for Orion's intended 24–48 hour holding period.

## Scope
The ranking engine combines active confidence, momentum, buy pressure,
controlled volatility, market regime, risk/reward and entry timing.

## Ownership
The ranking engine is advisory. It does not approve, size or execute trades.
Existing candidate eligibility remains unchanged. The score determines the
order in which eligible candidates are offered to the portfolio allocator.

## Next validation
Record ranking score and final trade outcome, then compare expectancy and
win rate per ranking band before changing eligibility thresholds.
