# Mission

Project Orion exists to provide traders with a professional deterministic desktop trading workstation for the United States equity market.

The platform assists traders by continuously analysing the market using deterministic algorithms and presenting only the most relevant opportunities.

Trading decisions always remain deterministic.

Artificial Intelligence exists exclusively to improve explainability.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision of Orion is to become a complete professional desktop trading workstation centred around **Mission Control**.

Mission Control should allow a trader to understand the current market within seconds after launching the application.

Instead of manually analysing hundreds of symbols, Orion continuously performs deterministic analysis and highlights only the opportunities that deserve attention.

---

# Product Philosophy

Project Orion follows one central philosophy:

> **The trader should understand the current market within seconds after opening Orion.**

Mission Control is therefore the operational centre of the application.

Every screen should present only information that directly supports trading decisions.

The desktop should feel closer to a Bloomberg Terminal than to a traditional desktop application.

---

# Core Principles

## Deterministic Decision Support

Every recommendation generated by Orion must be:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Reproducible
- Testable
- Transparent

TradingPipeline remains the only component allowed to determine BUY / HOLD / SELL recommendations.

---

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence improves understanding.

AI may:

- explain
- summarize
- compare
- translate deterministic output into natural language

AI never:

- creates BUY signals
- creates SELL signals
- creates EXIT signals
- calculates indicators
- determines confidence
- calculates position size
- overrides deterministic output

---

## Architecture First

Architecture always has priority over implementation speed.

Reusable services are preferred over feature-specific implementations.

Every new feature should naturally extend the existing architecture instead of introducing exceptions.

---

## Professional Engineering

Project Orion is developed as production-quality desktop software.

Every component should be:

- modular
- maintainable
- independently testable
- reusable

Business logic remains separated from presentation.

---
